## First Principles Analysis: what qualifies a person for an incident notification

**Deconstruction.** A notification obligation in v1.1 is made of four things fused into one cell: a fact about the incident (what, how bad, how it ended, where, who is involved), a role that cares about that fact, a channel and timing, and a response expectation (monitor, consider attending, attend). The type code was only ever a proxy for the first of these.
