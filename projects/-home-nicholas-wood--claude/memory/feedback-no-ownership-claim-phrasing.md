In positioning copy (LinkedIn headline, profile, bios), Nick rejected "own the problem end to end" and "I own the whole arc" as "out of place and try hard". He chose the option whose scope was shown through a verb arc: "I work the bigger problem, shape the strategy, then ship the system."

**Why:** Ownership claims assert status; verb arcs demonstrate it. Claiming ownership reads as trying to sound senior, which is the opposite of the effect. Same family as his cover-letter rules ([[cover-letter-voice]], power not gaps).

**How to apply:** In any self-positioning copy, never write "I own ..." or similar possession claims about problems, outcomes, or scope. Convey breadth with sequenced action verbs (work the problem, shape the strategy, ship the system) and let the reader infer ownership.
