Mid-way through an approved live-quote pull (2026-07-15, landlord insurance), Nick interrupted: "I don't want to submit forms, I want to collect information." This was AFTER he had said "yes get the live quotes" — his approval of an outcome did not extend to multi-step form submission as the method.

**Why:** Form funnels transmit his personal/property data step-by-step to third parties and are attributable to him. He wants information gathering (published rates, PDS facts, existing pages) separated from data-transmitting actions, which he does himself or approves per-step. Consistent with [[feedback-outbound-explicit-approval]] and the Interceptor PREFERENCES quick reference — treat multi-page quote/application funnels as a chain of outbound submits, not one approved action.

**How to apply:** When a task seems to need a web form (quotes, signups, applications), default to: collect what is publicly readable, prefill/draft at most, and surface the form to Nick to submit. If a funnel's early steps were already progressed, stop where you are, submit nothing further, and report what was entered. Ask before starting any funnel by naming the data it will transmit.
