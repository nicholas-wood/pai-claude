When Nick phrases a message as a question ("Should I...?", "Is it too short?", "Do you think...?"), he wants the recommendation, not the change applied. Even mid-iteration on a document with a deadline, a question is a decision point he is reserving for himself.

**Why:** During the Chris Jacobson Life Membership nomination (2026-07-18), he asked "Should I blend it into the first sentence instead?" and I answered AND applied the edits. He replied "I asked for your advice, not an update." Earlier in the same session the pattern worked correctly ("Is it too short?" → assessment only, he then directed). Momentum on execution requests does not transfer to questions.

**How to apply:** Distinguish instruction ("change X", "make it Y") from question ("should I...?", "what do you think?"). For questions, give recommendation first with reasoning, offer to apply it, and wait. Related: [[feedback-no-form-submission-collect-only]] (outcome approval is not funnel approval).
