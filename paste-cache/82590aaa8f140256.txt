Dear Members
 
It is now time to renew your membership at South Melbourne LSC for 2026-2027 season.
 
You need to do this via the SLSA Hub:  sls.com.au.  Please make sure that you have actually set up an account with your Username and password.  Make sure you re-register before making your payment.
 
·      Log into your account
·      Select season 2026/2027
·      Select the membership type you require
·      Make the payment.  This will be associated with your Membership Type.  If the correct Membership Type does not appear please contact the Membership Manager to change this.
 
Family Group Membership:  To renew a Family Group Membership please make sure that one of the adults in your group logs in to renew your memberships.
 
If you have any issues with the renewal process please contact the Membership Manager – Belinda Porter or 0493 688 428.
 
FOBs:  These will expire on 1st September 2026 and will be reinstated on membership renewal.
Once renewed you will receive an “Attestation” email that contains a link to complete the renewal.
You can apply for a FOB via this link: FOB application
