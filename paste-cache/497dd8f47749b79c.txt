What is an Incident?
A response by Life Saving Victoria members that either:
- Requires a response from multiple lifesaving services
    - Requires assistance from other Emergency Services including all situations where there is a danger to human life.
- Is otherwise serious or complex in nature such that members would like support, assistance, and visibility for LSV Command.

What is an Event?
An event is a major occurrence that result in, or has the potential to result in, multiple Incidents. An event is a way to associate multiple different incidents.

What is an Incident Type?
The nature of the situation that assets are responding to. An incident type should reflect the primary driver of a situation. E.g. A rescue followed by a First Aid would still be categorized as a rescue. A member presenting on the beach with a major first aid would be a medical incident. 

When should an Incident Type be updated?
- Further information has clarified what the primary situation is. I.e. The incident was miscategorised initially.
- The incident type should not be updated if the incident evolves over time. The incident type should still capture what we responded to.  (CONFIRM HOW AV DO THIS)

How do we capture evolving information for an Incident?
- Key information should be recorded in the incident log. 
- There are other key fields on an incident to capture data:
    - Agency Status
    - Incident Outcome (Dependent on Incident Type)
    - Incident Tags - Including but not limited to:
        - Resuscitation (FA Required)
        - Member Injury (FA Required)
        - Penthrane Administered (FA Required)

        - Oxygen Administered (FA Required)
